# The Centre - Site Activation Report
**Date:** December 2024
**Participants:** Rachel Atkinson (PICC CEO), Nic (A Curious Tractor), Sharon, Frank
**Project:** Initial site cleanup and assessment

---

## Executive Summary

Initial site activation phase successfully completed with young people from Palm Island. Major cleanup undertaken across all buildings and grounds, establishing foundation for future programs. Site now secure, safe, and ready for next phase development.

**Key Achievements:**
- 6 trailer loads + 3 skips of rubbish removed
- Two houses cleaned and made liveable
- All buildings secured with new locks
- Young people engaged and enthusiastic to return
- Site transformed from overgrown/unsafe to accessible

## Conclusion

The initial site activation has successfully transformed The Centre from an overgrown, unsafe, neglected property into a secure, clean, and accessible foundation for future programs.

The enthusiasm and capability demonstrated by young people, combined with strong community partnerships and solid infrastructure, positions the site for rapid program development.

With strategic investments in equipment and ongoing caretaker presence, The Centre is on track to become the thriving regional hub envisioned - bridging Palm Island and Townsville through youth pathways, circular economy manufacturing, and cultural connection.

**Key Success Factor:** The young people's engagement and pride in the transformation. They want to return, they're capable, and they're ready for the next phase.
